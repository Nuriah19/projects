Chicken Stew

This chicken is a delicacy all across kenya. Here's how to prepare it

Ingredients

  1. Kienyeji chicken
  2. Garlic
  3. Onion
  4. Tomatoes
  5. Salt to taste
  6. Cooking oil

Procedure

  1. Boil the chicken with salt,garlic and ginger till tender for 30 seconds
  2. In a pan heat coil till hot
  3. Add your boiled chicken in buthes.Fry till brown
  4. In a sufuria fry onions till it starts browning
  5. Add you chicken and stir
  6. Add blended tomaatoes and cover the sufuria
  7. Cook under low heat till tomatoes are fully cooked
  8. Add little water.Let it simmer for some minutes.
  9. Garnish with dhania
